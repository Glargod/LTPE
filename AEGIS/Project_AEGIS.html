1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Project AEGIS introduces the LTPE v6.3 navigation kernel featuring a Ritual Layer implemented as an Asynchronous Deterministic Fallback (ADF). This mechanism provides exceptional resilience in high-entropy, contested, and communications-denied environments by rapidly resolving logic entropy and reverting to pre-validated high-confidence behaviors when sensor fusion quality collapses.

Performance was validated through Monte Carlo Simulation of Simulation (SoS) runs (2,000 trials per condition), explicitly modeling interacting air and ground swarms with low-bandwidth LoS Hub data seeding under realistic uncertainties including sensor dropout and full EMCON.

2. CAPABILITY GAP ADDRESSED

The Canadian Armed Forces require reliable autonomous systems capable of operating in GPS-denied, communications-denied, and extreme cold environments (-40°C and below). Many current solutions suffer from catastrophic failure when sensor data integrity collapses due to electronic warfare, jamming, or environmental interference. AEGIS addresses this vulnerability through local line-of-sight decision-making and the Ritual Layer (ADF).

3. ARCHITECTURAL PILLARS

  • The Brain – LTPE v6.3 Navigation Kernel: A reactive, survival-oriented system using line-of-sight greedy planning, dead-end pruning, and the Ritual Layer (Asynchronous Deterministic Fallback) to maintain performance in high-chaos environments.
  • The Body – Distributed Sovereignty: Zonal 800V DC power architecture with Lithium Titanate (LTO) batteries housed in electromagnetically shielded compartments, rated for Arctic operations.
  • The Intelligence – Total Force Mesh: Low-bandwidth tactical mesh networking where designated air swarm LoS Hubs seed compressed situational awareness data to ground units.

5. VALIDATION – MONTE CARLO SIMULATION OF SIMULATION (SoS)

Validation was performed using a Monte Carlo SoS framework with 2,000 trials per scenario. Each trial simulates interacting air and ground swarms under randomized uncertainties, including sensor dropout and full EMCON conditions. Air swarm LoS Hubs provide low-bandwidth data seeding to the ground swarm in both test variants.
