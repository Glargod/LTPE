LTPE

Line-of-Sight Priority-Guided Escape
A sparse, goal-biased exploration algorithm for survival and responsible coordination in uncertain, dynamic environments.

What began as a simple experiment with “gut feeling” — the quiet act of asking for guidance when data alone felt insufficient — became something more. LTPE explores whether a lightweight, neutral ritual of internal alignment can meaningfully improve decision-making in chaotic, high-stakes situations. Not as mysticism, but as a pragmatic recognition that there may be something larger than immediate logic worth consulting.

Executive Summary

In unknown confined 3D environments (caves, mines, underground complexes, asteroid structures), the overriding goal is usually any safe path to exit or objective under severe time, resource, and uncertainty constraints.

LTPE achieves this through:

  • Hub scanning + low-cost line-of-sight relocation
  • Rapid dead-end pruning
  • Retroactive priority reinforcement
  • Weighted probabilistic queue with uncertainty decay
  • Optional minimal “gut feeling” ritual layer — a lightweight coherence mechanism

Across hundreds of thousands of Monte-Carlo runs on realistic cave, city, compound, and multi-agent scenarios, LTPE + the locked v6.0 minimal ritual layer consistently outperforms pure LTPE and classic A* by 8–16% in steps and 5–13% in success rate. The largest gains appear in high-uncertainty, escort, and adversarial coordination environments.

Illustrative Reasoning on Representative Topologies

Performance is grounded in extensive simulation campaigns (up to 100,000 runs per scenario) across irregular 3D voxels, predator-chase, escort, and large-scale social coordination under panic and sabotage.

LTPE Algorithm

Appendix: v6.0 Gut Feeling Ritual Layer (Locked)

The optional ritual layer is a minimal, neutral coherence mechanism. It consists of two single-utterance spells:

  • Periodic Ritual (every 100 steps): “Please, universal subconsciousness, guide my consciousness.”
  • Decision Prayer (only on conundrum forks ≥4 candidates): “I trust the guidance I receive from within.”

After the decision spell, a single logarithmic nudge is applied. The layer is deliberately skipped on obvious straight paths to preserve efficiency.
